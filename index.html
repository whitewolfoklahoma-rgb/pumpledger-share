      Help
      
  
      The short version of everything PumpLedger does.
      
  
      How does one job entry do four jobs?
      On the Log a Job tab you enter the customer, date, gallons, and disposal site once. That single record is the manifest (Manifests tab), a line in the customer's history (Customers tab), a line in the county tally (County Report tab), and it restarts that customer's pump-out clock (Due Soon tab). Nothing is re-typed anywhere.
      
  
      How are due dates figured?
      Each customer has a pump interval you set — 36 months is typical for a septic tank; grease traps are usually a few months, and many city grease programs set their own required cycle (verify yours). Due Soon counts from the customer's most recent pump record (inspections don't reset the clock). Change the interval on the customer and every date re-figures instantly.
      
  
      What exactly goes to the county?
      Whatever your county asks for — and that genuinely varies. The County Report tab totals gallons by disposal site, town, ZIP, and service for any date range and formats it as a clean report you can print or copy. It is a generic tally: confirm your county health department's current form, fields, and deadline before filing — PumpLedger does your numbers, you verify the form.
      
  
      Is the manifest official?
      The Manifests tab prints a complete, generic manifest from the job record — generator, transporter, disposal site, gallons, date, signatures. States, counties, and city FOG programs each have their own required form and copy rules (verify yours). If your program has an official form, the manifest still gives you every number for it in one place.
      
  
      How do reminders work?
      Due Soon lists everyone overdue or due within 90 days, worst first, each with a reminder letter written from their real last-pump date. Copy the letter into your mail or text app and send it your way — the demo sends nothing itself. Mark a customer reminded and the card shows the date, so nobody gets double-lettered.
      
  
      Where does my data live? How do I back it up?
      In this demo edition, everything lives on this device, in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Export the records CSV from County Report to keep a dated copy, and use Reset below to restore the fictional sample data or Clear to empty the app. Clearing your browser's site data erases your records, so export before you do.
